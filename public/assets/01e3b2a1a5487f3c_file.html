Frequently Asked Questions

Can Kresna integrate with our existing CRM or tools?

Yes. Kresna connects seamlessly with popular CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive, along with your favorite communication and reporting tools.

Can Kresna help automate our follow-ups?

Yes. Kresna automatically schedules follow-ups, reminders, and tasks so your team never misses a lead again.

Does Kresna support team collaboration?

Absolutely. You can share notes, assign tasks, and track progress together, keeping everyone aligned in real time.

Is my data secure with Kresna?

Yes. We use end-to-end encryption and enterprise-grade security standards to protect your business data.

Can we customize Kresna to fit our workflow?

Yes. Kresna is built to adapt — you can tailor automations, AI suggestions, and reporting to match your exact process.

Do I need any technical skills to set it up?

Not at all. Kresna has a simple, no-code setup. You can connect your tools and launch automations in minutes.
